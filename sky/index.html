01 / WHY WE GO

走進山林，Step into the forest,
也走近真實的人。and closer to real lives.

當熟悉的道路改變，When familiar roads change,
留在這裡的人，如何繼續生活？how do those who stay carry on?

地震與崩塌，讓中橫沿線的交通、觀光與日常面臨改變。這次天之校，我們帶著對土地與人的關心，走進中橫，聽居民、店家、第一線工作者與生態研究者，說出各自正在經歷的生活。Earthquakes and landslides have changed transport, tourism and daily life along the Central Cross-Island Highway, known as Zhongheng. With care for the land and its people, Sky School will listen to residents, shopkeepers, frontline workers and ecology researchers share what they are experiencing.

以訪談與現場觀察為起點，透過 AI 協作與敏捷工作流程，把聽見的故事，做成有脈絡、有溫度的影像紀錄。Starting with interviews and field observation, we will use AI collaboration and Agile workflows to create thoughtful films that preserve the context and humanity of each story.

本頁呈現活動學習規劃；沿線現況將由師生實地訪查記錄。圖片包含主辦方提供的場域參考照與歷次活動素材，非本次即時影像。This page presents the learning plan. Students and mentors will document conditions through field visits. 02 / THREE QUESTIONS, MANY LIVES

在一條路上，找到真實生活與生命的答案One road. Questions about everyday life and the choices we make.

01

路變了，日常怎麼走？When roads change, how does daily life adapt?

從通勤、通學到就醫與物資運補，聽見交通變動如何進入每一天，也看見維護道路與公共服務的人。From commuting and school journeys to medical care and supplies, explore how transport changes shape each day, and meet the people who maintain roads and public services.

「現在出門一趟，和以前有什麼不同？」“How is going out today different from before?”
02

客人少了，生活怎麼過？With fewer visitors, how do people make a living?

從店家、旅宿到山區農業，理解觀光與運輸的變化，如何牽動工作、生計與社區之間的支持。Through shops, accommodation and mountain farming, explore how changes in tourism and transport affect jobs, livelihoods and community support.

「這段時間，你做了哪些調整？」“What adjustments have you made during this time?”
03

為什麼，還是選擇留下？Why choose to stay?

在人的故事與森林生態之間，看見對家鄉的情感、與自然共處的經驗，以及面對變動的生命選擇。Through personal stories and forest ecology, discover ties to home, experiences of living with nature and life choices in times of change.

「是什麼，讓你繼續在這裡生活？」“What keeps you living here?”

03 / LEARN IN SHORT CYCLES

用 AI 整理線索，Use AI to organise the clues.
用敏捷讓故事成形。Use Agile to shape the story.

在徐柏峯教練帶領下，把大任務拆成可以完成的小步驟。每一輪拍攝後一起檢視、調整，再帶著更好的問題回到現場。With coach Xu Baifeng, break a large task into achievable steps. Review and adjust after each filming round, then return to the field with better questions.

  1. 01
    觀察與準備｜找到值得問的問題Observe & Prepare | Find questions worth asking

    整理背景資料、建立訪綱、分配採訪與拍攝任務；AI 協助梳理問題與資訊缺口。Research the background, develop interview outlines and assign interviewing and filming roles. AI helps clarify questions and gaps in information.

  2. 02
    走訪與拍攝｜讓當事人說自己的故事Visit & Film | Let people tell their own stories

    經受訪者同意後，以影片記錄訪談與生活現場，學習提問、聆聽、構圖與收音。With interviewees' consent, record interviews and everyday settings while practising questioning, listening, composition and sound recording.

  3. 03
    整理與回饋｜每天完成一小步Organise & Review | Make progress every day

    運用 AI 協助逐字稿、素材分類與故事架構；團隊以短週期檢視進度，核對引述與脈絡。Use AI to assist with transcripts, footage organisation and story structure. Review progress in short cycles and check quotations and context.

  4. 04
    剪輯與分享｜完成一段有依據的敘事Edit & Share | Build a story grounded in evidence

    在專業指導下完成分鏡與剪輯，透過放映和回顧，說明自己的觀察與學習。With professional guidance, develop shot sequences and edit the film. Share your observations and learning through screenings and reflection.

西寶、洛韶、慈恩，生活仍在這條路上。Life continues in Xibao, Luoshao and Ci'en.

沿線經過的地方，有孩子上學的校園、居民生活的聚落，也有持續耕作與經營的果園。我們想靠近這些日常，聽人們說說與土地的連結，以及留下的選擇。Along the route are schools, residential communities and working orchards. We hope to listen to people's connections with the land and their decisions to stay.

花蓮・學者與環境工作者Hualien · Researchers & Environmental Practitioners

擬邀黃斐悅、許育誠、吳海音、黃文彬、王元均等交流，從鳥類、野生動物、水域生態、環境參與與生物多樣性，看見人與自然的關係。Prospective guests include Huang Feiyue (黃斐悅), Xu Yucheng (許育誠), Wu Haiyin (吳海音), Huang Wenbin (黃文彬) and Wang Yuanjun (王元均). Explore relationships between people and nature through birds, wildlife, aquatic ecology, environmental participation and biodiversity.

太魯閣・保育與復原Taroko · Conservation & Recovery

擬訪太魯閣國家公園管理單位，了解災後復原、保育、遊憩管理與地方生活之間的協調。We plan to approach Taroko National Park management to learn how post-disaster recovery, conservation, recreation management and local life are coordinated.

天祥・店家與旅宿Tianxiang · Shops & Accommodation

擬訪補給店家、餐飲經營者、天祥青年活動中心與太魯閣晶英酒店工作者，理解客源、運補與工作的改變。Prospective visits include supply shops, food businesses, Tianxiang Youth Activity Center and staff at Silks Place Taroko, to understand changes in visitors, supplies and work.

西寶與洛韶・聚落與學校Xibao & Luoshao · Communities & Schools

擬透過在地夥伴接洽居民、有機農業工作者及西寶國小，聆聽耕作、通學、社區互助與留在家鄉的故事。Through local partners, we plan to contact residents, organic farmers and Xibao Elementary School to hear stories of farming, school journeys, mutual support and staying in one's hometown.

慈恩與關原・第一線日常Ci'en & Guanyuan · Life on the Front Line

慈恩何家果園列為沿線觀察場域；另擬訪山莊、關原加油站、道路養護及警政服務人員，理解山區農業、高山補給、交通維護與生活支持。The He family orchard in Ci'en is a planned observation site. Other prospective interviews include lodge staff, Guanyuan fuel station staff, road maintenance workers and police, exploring farming, mountain supplies, road upkeep and everyday support.

觀雲山莊・在高山一起整理故事Guanyun Lodge · Bringing Stories Together in the Mountains

規劃作為住宿與團隊整理素材的節點，也從用水、能源、物資與廢棄物，看見高山旅宿的日常課題。Planned as an accommodation and teamwork base, the lodge also offers a perspective on water, energy, supplies and waste in mountain hospitality.

MOUNTAIN AWARENESS / 山林・五感・內在連結MOUNTAIN AWARENESS / Forests, Senses & Inner Connection

在三千多公尺的高山，Above 3,000 metres,
貼近聆聽山巒的心跳。listen closely to the heartbeat of the mountains.

走入老樹林間與草地，Walk among old trees and across grasslands.
打開五感，感受大地的能量。Awaken your senses and feel the energy of the earth.

這趟旅程，我們也把時間留給山林與自己。放慢腳步，在老樹之間、草地之上，留意風的聲音、林間的氣息、光影的變化，以及身體此刻的感受。This journey also makes time for the forest and for yourself. Slow down among old trees and on the grass. Notice the wind, forest scents, shifting light and the sensations in your body.

透過靜坐冥想，讓注意力回到呼吸與內在，Through meditation, return your attention to your breath and inner world.
在感受山林的同時，也重新與自己連結。As you experience the forest, reconnect with yourself.

由五感覺察老師李樂翌帶領 ↓Guided by sensory awareness teacher Li Yueyi ↓
  1. 01
    走入山林｜讓感官慢慢打開Enter the Forest | Let Your Senses Awaken

    走進老樹林間與草地，用眼、耳、鼻、口與身體感受當下，練習覺察自己與環境的關係。Walk among old trees and grasslands. Use sight, hearing, smell, taste and bodily awareness to notice the present and your relationship with the environment.

  2. 02
    貼近大地｜聽見山巒的心跳Connect with the Earth | Hear the Mountains' Heartbeat

    在高山的遼闊裡，感受風、樹、土地與生命的節奏，讓日常匆忙的步調慢下來。In the openness of the high mountains, sense the rhythms of wind, trees, earth and life, and let the rush of everyday life slow down.

  3. 03
    靜坐冥想｜與內在的自己相遇Sit & Meditate | Meet Your Inner Self

    在引導下練習靜坐與冥想，回到呼吸，覺察身心與情緒，為自己留下一段安靜聆聽的時間。Practise guided sitting and meditation. Return to your breath, notice your body, mind and emotions, and give yourself time to listen quietly.

把這份覺察帶回訪談與影像創作：更細緻地看見山林，更專注地聽人說話，也更清楚地感受自己。Bring this awareness into interviews and filmmaking: observe the forest more closely, listen to people more attentively and understand your own feelings more clearly.
